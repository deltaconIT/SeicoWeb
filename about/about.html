SEICO overview

SEICO (Steel Buildings and Equipment Industries JSC) was established in 2003 with the wave of investment from Japan and industrialized countries. We are specialized in the field of design, fabrication and construction of steel structures, steel buildings for many industries, civil, transportation, trade and defense across the country. Through the establishment and development, SEICO has become a truly reliable partner of many construction companies as well as major domestic and international investors.

We understand that every steel building product has special technical requirements. In our experience, we analyze and synthesize the requirements to give customers optimum choice in a wide range of materials for all individual structures such as frame, roof, wall, EPS/PU/Rockwool Panel, Welded wire mesh, Deck and Truss Deck with best price and quality.

In addition, SEICO also provides customers with a wide range of auxiliary products for color and durability improvement such as special Alkyd paints, Epoxy-Polyurethane outdoor paint, durable paint in marine environments, and other specialized paint lines such as factory floor paint, garage, basement, painting walls, fire protection.

SEICO currently operates four state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, equipped with advanced production technologies and modern machinery.

Each year, we deliver more than 60,000 tons of structural steel, 2 million square meters of roofing and wall cladding systems, millions of square meters of composite floor decking and Truss Deck systems, as well as nearly 30 million liters of industrial coatings to serve a wide range of industrial, commercial, and infrastructure projects.

Our manufacturing excellence is supported by internationally recognized management systems, including ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, and EN ISO 3834-2:2021. ISO Quality Management System

Health

SEICO always strives to create a healthy and safe working environment for our staff. Our employees are regularly trained on the health and safety requirements for their work. At the same time, our production facilities are operated under strict technical and administrative systems, regular maintenance programs and safe work procedures.

Inspection work is carried out periodically. Health and safety policies and guidelines issued by the government are updated regularly to reflect current health and safety conditions. These principles include hazardous materials management and workplace injury reduction.

The objective of SEICO is to implement reasonable and scientific working conditions to ensure the health of employees and in accordance with the law:

  • Our company fully implements policies for employees in accordance with current regulations of the government: social insurance, health insurance for 100% of employees. We also organize employees' conferences annually. In 2018, the company reviewed and rewarded excellent units and individuals for fulfilling their duties.
  • Working time: 8 hours / day (48 hours / week).
  • Holidays, New Year: according to the current regulations of the government.
  • Sickness, maternity, health care leave: according to the current regulations of the government.
  • Working conditions: SEICO is equipped with adequate protective equipment and facilities for laborers to work. We constantly update any changes in regulations and procedures on occupational safety and health.

Safety

Establishing health and environment safety to protect the "Labour Source" is everyone's conscience and responsibility. In which, the highest level of responsibility belongs to senior managers assigned to the task of occupational safety.

Direct supervisor

  • Direct supervisor is required to monitor the workplace on daily basis to ensure a safe work area to avoid risks to employee health and safety.
  • The supervisor must be present throughout the working time.
  • The supervisor must be familiar with the process that is directly relevant to the task being monitored.
  • Upon detection of life-threatening or defective equipment, they have the right to suspend work and request other workers to follow their instructions.

Worker

  • The worker is responsible for performing work at the workplace in accordance with the employer's safety standards and relevant rules and regulations.
  • Temporary workers need to comply with the standards at workplace and the instructions of assigned supervisor.

Environment

Working environment management

Untidy workplaces can cause accidents, inefficiencies, and risk. The environment which is not clean and safe will affect the health of each person, so cleaning the workplace is necessary to make people feel comfortable and help them focus better on their work.

  • Everyone has the responsibility to ensure a clean, tidy workplace. Working equipment must be located in a safe, secure and properly regulated area.
  • When grinding, metal pieces or small pieces must be stored in a regulated area to avoid missing.
  • The work area must be clean and tidy before commencement of work.
  • All work areas must be inspected regularly; leftover materials must be removed or stacked daily.
  • All fuel, hazardous or chemical spills must be cleaned up. When smoking, cigarette butts and rubbish should be put into appropriate containers.

Why choose SEICO?

SEICO Steel Building and Equipment Industries Joint Stock Company provides you with a complete solution for pre-engineered steel buildings, steel structures including design, manufacturing, erection and warranty. This enclosed process brings many benefits to our customers with optimal solutions, high quality products and perfect service.

Optimal design

Thanks to our experienced and well-trained design team, with undergraduate and postgraduate degrees and the help of automation design software, SEICO promises to deliver the best possible project solution to customers.

Assured quality

All components of the prefabricated steel structure are manufactured at the factory with the ISO 9001:2015 quality management system in addition to the continuous and thorough control of the quality assurance department. SEICO products are therefore guaranteed to meet international quality standards.

Quick installation

Steel structures and auxiliary components are made available at the factory and transported to the site, thus the installation process takes place quickly and easily. This greatly reduces construction time compared to conventional construction methods.
