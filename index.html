SEICO (Steel Frame Building & Industrial Equipment JSC)

About seico

Founded in 2003, SEICO specializes in the design, fabrication and construction of steel structures and pre-engineered steel buildings. SEICO supplies not only steel structures but total solutions for projects: floor sheets, roofing, welded steel mesh and industrial paint. With 4 factories in Hai Phong, a head office in Hanoi and representative offices in Vietnam and Southeast Asia, SEICO effectively serves large factory, workshop, warehouse, logistics center and infrastructure projects in Vietnam and exports to many countries. Known for its capability and experienced engineers, SEICO has become a trusted partner of investors, FDI companies and domestic and international contractors.
