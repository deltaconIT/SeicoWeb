Pre-engineered steel buildings is a highly versatile product in today's construction industry. It has features that attract investors, such as high durability, wide applicability, easy dismantling and reuse, fast construction and low cost. Pre-engineered steel buildings are used in every sector, from agriculture to industry, commerce and public works.

 

Some applications of pre-engineered steel buildings:

01   Agriculture

 

Livestock and poultry farms, greenhouses, warehouses, poultry houses, farms, organic plants…

02   Industrial

 

Manufacturing plants, industrial factories, steel mills, warehouses, assembly plants…

03   Commercial

 

Multi-purpose buildings, shopping centers, restaurants, offices, …

04   Other Projects

 

Schools, training centers, hospitals, conference centers, laboratories, museums, churches…
